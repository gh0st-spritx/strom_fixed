5 · Induction

Weak: basis P(n₀); step P(k)→P(k+1).

Strong: basis P(n₀); step [P(n₀)∧..∧P(k)]→P(k+1).

Structural: basis-step elements ✓; recursive step: pieces true ⟹ built element true.

well-ordering ⟺ induction ⟺ strong induction

Recursive defn = basis value + recursive rule (+ implicit exclusion).
